WHO WE ARE

Mint Petroleum Ltd is a Zambian-owned and operated petroleum company established to provide reliable, high-quality fuel and energy solutions to businesses and industries across the country. We specialise in the supply and distribution of transport fuels, industrial commercial fuels and lubrication products, serving sectors that form the backbone of Zambia's economy.

Our operations are built on a foundation of consistency, professionalism and a customer-first approach. We work closely with our clients to understand their unique energy needs and deliver tailored solutions that support operational efficiency, cost management and long-term growth.

As a proudly Zambian company, we are deeply committed to contributing to the nation's economic development. Every aspect of our business—from sourcing and logistics to customer service and safety—is managed with the highest standards of integrity and excellence.

"Reliability is not a promise we make—it is the standard by which we operate."

HEALTH, SAFETY,SECURITY
& ENVIRONMENT (HSSE)

At Mint Petroleum Ltd, we believe that operational excellence begins with a strong commitment to Health, Safety, Security and Environmental (HSSE) responsibility. Every aspect of our business is guided by practices that protect our people, our customers, our assets and the communities in which we operate.

We are committed to maintaining a safe working environment by promoting a proactive safety culture, encouraging personal responsibility and ensuring that every task is carried out with the highest regard for health and safety. Through continuous training, risk awareness and operational discipline, we strive to prevent incidents before they occur.

Environmental stewardship is equally central to our operations. We support responsible resource management and continually seek opportunities to minimise environmental impact through safe product handling, efficient operations and compliance with applicable environmental standards and regulations.

Security remains an essential part of our commitment to protecting our people, products and infrastructure. We maintain appropriate measures to safeguard our operations while ensuring the reliable and uninterrupted delivery of energy solutions to our customers.

At Mint Petroleum Ltd, HSSE is more than a business requirement—it is a shared responsibility and a core value that shapes every decision we make.

➔ Our HSSE Commitments

  • Safety First We place the safety and wellbeing of people at the centre of every operation.
  • Risk Management We proactively identify, assess and manage risks to prevent incidents.
  • Environmental Responsibility We promote environmentally responsible practices that minimise our operational footprint.
  • Regulatory Compliance We conduct our operations in accordance with applicable laws, regulations and industry standards.
  • Continuous Improvement We regularly review and strengthen our systems to improve safety, efficiency and operational performance.
  • Shared Responsibility Every employee, contractor and business partner contributes to maintaining a safe and responsible workplace.

"Every safe operation reflects our commitment to our people, our customers and the communities we serve."
